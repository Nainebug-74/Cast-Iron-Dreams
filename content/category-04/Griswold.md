# Griswold Manufacturing

## Overview-
Grisowld Manufacturing was a world renowned American maker of cast iron cookware.
 Griswold is highly prized by collectors due to it's light weight and smooth finish. The cookware has a very distinctive logo making it easy to identify. Several of the pieces are rare and highly  
 sought after and can be expensive to purchase.

## Griswold Ownership
The manufacturing company was retained by the family until 1946 when it was sold to an investment company. Later that same year it was sold to [[Wagner]]. Wagner continued to manufacture Griswold products until 1957.

## The Griswold Logo

1. Slanted Logo was used until the early 1920s 
2.  A block lettering logo was used until the 1930s
3.  In the 1940s -1957 they produced a small logo
