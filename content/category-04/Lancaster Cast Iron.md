## Overview

Lancaster Cast Iron started in 2018, founded by Mark Longnecker and Brandon Moore. After a year of researching foundries and machine shops they launched their No. 8 skillet in 2019. They operate a retail store in a historic schoolhouse in Conestoga, Pennsylvania. Their skillets are sand-cast and seasoned locally working with a small team and local makers. The cookware comes with a lifetime warranty and contains no PFAS or synthetic coatings. This newer line of cookware can cost as much as some of the collectible [[Lodge]], [[Wagner]], and [[Griswold]] pieces without the history.


## Known for
1. Lightweight , a No 8 can weigh **4-4.5 lbs** , but retains vintage inspired details like pour spouts and teardrop handles.
2. Hand seasoned with Grapeseed oil these can be used on gas, electric, inductions, and even open flame stoves.
3. Hand carved wooden utensils, cutting boards, and hand crafted leather goods are part of their charm when you shop online.
