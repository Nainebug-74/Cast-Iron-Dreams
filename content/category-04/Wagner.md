## Wagner Ware

## Overview-
**Wagner Ware** produced iconic aluminum and cast iron cookware from 1891-1990s.
Today pieces from the early to mid 20th century are considered prize collectors items.
As shown here you can expect adding a pre-1952 piece to your collection to be an expensive investment.

![[wagner-prices.png]]

## Ownership
The family retained ownership of the business until 1952 when it was sold to the Randall Company. This  is often considered the end of their Golden Era by collectors. In the 1990s domestic foundry operations wound close for good. There was a brief attempt to continue using overseas production that was short lived and not well received.

## Notable Variations
1. For a brief period after absorbing the [[Griswold]] brand they did produce a  "**Wagwold**" marking showing both makers marks on their pans.
2. They produced store-branded pieces such as **Wardway** for Montgomery Wards.
