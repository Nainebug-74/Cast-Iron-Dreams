## Cocinaware


## Overview-
Cocinaware is a modern cast iron that is only available at HEB retail stores. It is a signature part of their Mexican-inspired and contemporary Tex-Mex cookware, kitchen gadgets, and tableware. The signature pan collection is cast by a foundry in Columbia. When researching it is noted that the exact foundry is never named and there has been some deep diving conspiracy threads on Reddit discussing exactly what foundry makes the cookware.

## Ownership
As part of an exclusive line by HEB this cookware is owned and sold only by HEB and the Butts family. The stores are limited to Texas and Mexico.

## Notable variations
1. All of the Cocinaware cast iron is pre-seasoned and has a smooth finish.
2. The new pans often have a blue/purple color caused by the intense heat used when seasoning. As you use, clean, and re-season the pan it becomes the traditional deep black associated with cast iron.
3. Affordable pricing has made this line very popular with naturalist looking to get away from the coatings and chemicals used for most non-stick cookware. They are even more affordable then [[Lodge]].
